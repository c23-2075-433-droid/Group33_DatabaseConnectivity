WHY THIS ADDENDUM IS BEING SUBMITTED

After the validation form was reviewed and signed on 12 November 2026, the researchers audited the game's fifteen built scenes against the word list that had been submitted. The audit found that the list covered 46 of the 53 Filipino words the game actually asks the child to speak. Nine words are asked for in the built game but were not presented for validation, and two words on the validated list are not yet implemented.

The omission was the researchers' error in preparing the original list, not a change made afterwards. This addendum is submitted so that the validation covers the vocabulary as the children will encounter it during testing. No word already approved on 12 November is being changed or withdrawn by this document.

PART 2 — THREE RULINGS REQUESTED ON STRESS MARKS

The original form explained that a mark is shown to the child only where the bare spelling would be two different words, and listed five such pairs: Tayô, Upô, Bukás, Bása, Bátì. Three questions remain.

1. TALÓN — is it a marked pair?
The game currently shows this word to the child with its mark, as talón (to jump), to distinguish it from tálon (a waterfall). It appears unmarked on the original form, so the form and the game disagree. Please confirm which is correct.

2. TÁPON — the ruling raised on the original form.
The original form proposed tápon (to throw away), not to be read as tapón (a cork or stopper), and left it for your ruling. If this was answered in your written comments, please disregard; if not, a ruling is requested.

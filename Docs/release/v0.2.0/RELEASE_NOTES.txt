WHAT CHANGED SINCE v0.1.1
-------------------------

Stress check for six words (the main change).
  Tayo, Upo, Bukas, Basa, Bati and Pasok are each two different words
    depending on stress (ta-YO "stand up" / TA-yo "we", and so on). The
    speech recognizer returns letters only, so it cannot tell them apart.
  For these six words the game now also judges HOW the word was said:
    - PronunciationModel, an on-device classifier (one logistic regression
      per word) on five measurements taken inside the child's own word:
      vowel-length ratio, pitch difference, loudness difference, pitch fall
      in the last vowel, and a whole-word comparison (dynamic time warping)
      with recorded speakers. Absolute pitch, loudness and speed never count.
    - Trained on 229 recordings from five adult speakers, each heard under
      12 simulated conditions (room noise, raised child-like voices, slower
      and faster speech).
    - The older Rachel-based judges are kept as a safety net.
  Three answers: Correct, Other word (counted as a miss), or Say it again
    (not counted). Every other word is judged by the recognized text alone,
    exactly as in v0.1.1.
  Audio bridge (Android 13+): a small Android plugin
    (Assets/Plugins/Android/SalinlahiAudioRecognizer.java) records the child,
    keeps the audio in memory for the classifier, and hands the same audio to
    Android's speech recognizer. Nothing is written to storage or uploaded by
    the game. Phones that refuse it fall back, for the session, to normal
    listening and a rougher loudness-based check.
  Results, leave one speaker out, whole game check (Docs/PronunciationChecking.md
    section 2h): correct pronunciation accepted 95/117, other pronunciation
    refused 81/112, 2 wrongly accepted, 2 wrongly refused, 49 asked again;
    77% of all takes decided correctly (98% of decided takes). The earlier
    Rachel-only check on the same takes: 75/117, 87/112, 2, 5, 60; 71%.
    The decision lines were tuned on the same recordings, so these figures
    are optimistic. No Grade 2-3 learner has been recorded or tested.
  Bati is now written Bati with one mark (BA-ti); v0.1.1 wrote it with two.
    The group's validation form uses the two-mark spelling; this is pending
    the validator's confirmation.

Nicknames.
  A name is now checked against the other profiles on the device, then
    claimed online (Supabase nickname_claims), so two children cannot share
    one. The claim sends the nickname and a random installation ID. If the
    check cannot be completed, the name is accepted, as before.

Voice and narration.
  34 of Rachel's word clips were replaced with clearer takes, and Pagkain now
    has its recording. 471 voice clips (v0.1.1: 468); 13 voice-bank entries
    are empty (v0.1.1: 14).
  Rachel's lip sync is baked from the recordings (Rhubarb) instead of the TTS
    timings; clips not yet baked are lip-synced live.

Scenes.
  Paaralan 5: Tayo, Kunin, Paalam and Alis are drawn at the desk's scale; Alis
    walks off and the room fades before the summary appears. Rachel says
    Ligpit there, not Linis.

Measurement.
  The voice-attempt log gains 11 columns: 5 for recognition latency and 6 for
    the stress check. It still holds text and numbers only, never audio, and
    is never uploaded.
  PerfSampler measures frames per second, memory and startup on the device.

Tools (not in the APK).
  Tools/pronunciation_dataset: cut, label and import new speakers'
    recordings. Tools/test_stress_rules.sh: the tests and the
    leave-one-speaker-out evaluation, without Unity.


KNOWN ISSUES
------------

  THE STRESS CHECK IS NOT VALIDATED FOR CHILDREN. Trained and tested on five
    adult speakers; children's voices were only simulated. Its accuracy for
    Grade 2-3 learners is unknown. Do not present it as a pronunciation
    assessment.

  "Say it again" is common on the six words (about one answer in five in the
    evaluation), and there is no limit on how often it can repeat for one
    word. Nothing is counted against the child, but a child could be asked
    again and again.

  The audio bridge has been tried on one phone (Galaxy A03s, Android 13).
    Its on-device recognizer was not available, so the standard one was used.
    [CONFIRM: result of the release test on this APK, below.]

  On Android 8-12 the six words use the loudness-based fallback, which is
    less precise and may ask again more often.

  Speech recognition goes through the device's standard recognizer, which may
    process the audio online. The game's own classifier is on the device.

  Kinig and Tapon have no recording of the word itself. Eight more empty
    word entries (Suot, Padyak, Alok, Apir, Hugas, Sali, Tara, Salamin) are not
    asked in any scene. 13 empty voice-bank entries in all.

  The scene badges show plain spellings (Tayo, Bati) except in Klasrum, while
    the word sign above shows stress marks (TAYO with its mark). Two
    spellings of the same word can be on screen together.

  Unchanged from v0.1.1: the long silent pause before the first word of a
    scene; MINI GAME does nothing; a word badge sits low in Paaralan 5;
    suspend and resume not tested at full volume; no desktop voice input; the
    leaderboard cannot be filtered to one child.

INSTALLATION / RUNNING INSTRUCTIONS
-----------------------------------

See README.txt in this folder. In short:

  1. v0.1.0 or v0.1.1 can be upgraded in place.
  2. Copy SALINLAHI-v0.2.0.apk to an Android 8.0+ device and open it.
  3. Allow installation from this source, then allow the microphone.
  4. Stay online, turn the volume up, and say the Filipino word on screen.

Confirm the build by opening SETTINGS > ABOUT; it should read v0.2.0.

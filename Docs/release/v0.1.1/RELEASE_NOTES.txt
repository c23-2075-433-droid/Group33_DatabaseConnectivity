WHAT CHANGED SINCE v0.1.0
-------------------------

Teacher Rachel is no longer silent anywhere.
  The 15 repeated lines that had no recording in v0.1.0 are recorded and
    wired: the framing lines ("Sabihin", "Ngayon", "Ulitin"), the listening
    cue, both hints, the idle nudge, the mic-blocked line, and the three
    correct / two incorrect / two not-heard replies. These are the lines
    heard most often in a session, and they were the loudest gap in v0.1.0.
  The 17 lines that were still being read in English are now spoken in
    Filipino.
  468 voice clips, up from 423. 468 of the voice bank's 482 entries are
    wired; the 14 that are not are listed under Known Issues.
  Her portrait is drawn in the narration panel and her mouth moves while she
    speaks, driven by the narrator's own state rather than a timer, so she
    stops the instant a line is cut off. In v0.1.0 the ring framed the room
    behind her because no sprite was assigned.

Vocabulary and objectives.
  51 distinct Filipino words across 72 spoken objectives in 15 scenes, with
    21 of the objectives re-asking a word taught earlier. This agrees with
    3.2.4 and 3.6 of the paper and with Addendum A to the vocabulary
    validation; it was counted from the `objectives` array of each scene's
    SceneObjectiveController in the v0.1.1 scene files, which is the list the
    game actually walks through.
  Parke's full word list is back. A restructure on 9-12 November had cut the
    five Parke builders down to 19 objectives; the scene files had been kept
    on the fuller list, and re-running the builders on 17 November took eight
    words out of the game - Tingin, Tuwa, Tulak, Kumusta, Laro, Ibahagi,
    Pagod and Talon. Talon mattered most: it is the one worked example quoted
    in 1.1 of the paper, and for a few hours it was a word with nothing to be
    the answer to. The builders and the scenes now agree again.
  The home chapter ends at breakfast. The sixth Bahay scene, "Leaving for
    School", was designed but never built and sat on the map as a locked
    tile; it is off the map now, along with the Palengke marker. The journey
    therefore no longer ends on a tile nobody can enter.
  Kanin and Itlog are no longer separate objectives: "Pagkain" brings the
    whole meal out and "Linis" clears it away.

Pronunciation checking - tightened, and the phone agreed.
  The whole word now has to be said. Saying the first syllable - "ba" for
    "Bangon" - used to pass; it does not now.
  An accepted variant has to be the whole recognizer result, not a substring
    of it.
  The editor's own emulator no longer answers on the child's behalf, which
    was masking failures during development.
  The stress mark came off Kinig, which is not one of the ambiguous pairs.
  The tenth release check, which v0.1.0 failed, PASSED on 17 November: a word
    said wrong on purpose was refused on a real phone and the prompt asked
    again. Read the entry under Known Issues before treating this as settled -
    it is one phone and a handful of words, which is evidence, not proof.

Animation and sound.
  Bahay 3 rebuilt around the bath: Kylo faces the child, brushes the teeth
    that are actually there, combs hair that moves, and drips instead of
    changing his hair to show he is wet. The bath hides the change it makes.
  Bahay 4: the folded uniform is cut from the painted art and sits on the
    flat top of the mattress, at the socks' real size. The bag comes out on
    the answer rather than on the question, and is picked up as its own beat.
  Bahay 5: Kylo sits on a drawn sitting pose instead of a standing picture
    slid down, sized to the chair, and eats and drinks with real recorded
    sounds - smacks for chewing, glugs for drinking. The Pagkain badge is on
    the table, not on his chest.
  Paaralan 1: the guard pushes the gate open in six frames, the gate hinges
    on its jambs, Kylo turns before he walks through, and the school bell he
    is running to beat rings when he beats it.
  Paaralan 3: Kylo writes, with one pencil instead of two.
  Paaralan 4: Kylo eats with a mouth that moves, on sandwich art drawn for it.
  Paaralan 5 has moved into Paaralan 4's classroom, and its five objectives
    are recorded through: Alis, Kunin, Ligpit, Paalam and Tayo. Upo is no
    longer asked for there; Tayo is, which is the word the new staging needs.
  Paaralan 4: the lesson desk is animated for Ligpit, Kuha, Kain, Inom and
    Tapon, and the Ligpit pencil is carried into the bag on the pose's own
    clock.
  Ten seconds of silence brings out crickets - three quarters of a second,
    stoppable - and a wordless "yes" and "oops" answer right and wrong.
  Kylo no longer swells by a fifth every time he waves, and breathing no
    longer undoes the walk.

Screen and layout.
  The background covers screens wider than 16:9 instead of letterboxing.
  Landscape either way up.
  The bathroom cup is back to the size the room was painted around.
  The summary panel says TOTAL ATTEMPTS on the plate that always counted
    tries, and the words-learned board prints the word in front of the slash.
  The recent-scores list no longer prints through the Replay and Continue
    buttons.

Main menu and leaderboard.
  The About popup is the drawn board it was given, and the leaderboard closes
    on an X in its corner rather than an OKAY button underneath it.
  Both boards scroll, so ten children sharing a tablet can all find their
    names. Per-player filtering is still not there.
  Volume settings are applied when a gameplay scene is entered directly, not
    only on the one Start() in the main menu.

Under the hood.
  Parke's SupabaseClient has the config the other levels have.
  The save file, the publishable key and the package are treated as
    untrusted input; 19 input-validation rules are enforced and tested.
  Tools/secret_scan.sh and Tools/test_security_rules.sh both pass clean on
    this source: no credential in anything tracked, 19 of 19 rules holding.
  .gitattributes stops Git rewriting the PDFs as text on Windows.


KNOWN ISSUES
------------

  WRONG PRONUNCIATION IS REJECTED ON A PHONE NOW - ON ONE PHONE, ON THE WORDS
    THAT WERE TRIED. This was the most serious issue in v0.1.0 and the state
    of it has changed, so read what the change is and is not.
    What happened: the device test on 17 November deliberately mispronounced a
    word - a swapped vowel, the same kind of mistake v0.1.0 waved through - and
    the game refused it and asked again. v0.1.0 accepted it.
    What that means: the feared explanation did not bite here. It was thought
    the device's Filipino dictation service might be returning the nearest real
    Filipino word rather than the sounds the child made, handing the checker an
    already-corrected word so the mistake never reached it. On this phone, with
    this word, the mistake reached the checker and the checker caught it. The
    tightening in this release - whole word required, a variant having to be
    the whole recognizer result - is doing something real on a device, not only
    in its own unit tests.
    What that does NOT mean: that the system is proven to correct pronunciation.
    One phone, one recognizer, one Android version, a handful of words. The
    recognizer's behaviour varies by device, by language pack and by how noisy
    the room is, and the v0.1.0 failure was found by trying exactly once. Before
    Chapter 4 or the defense claims the game corrects a child's pronunciation,
    this check wants running across several words, on at least one other phone,
    with VoiceAttemptLog read back to see what the recognizer actually returned
    each time. That log is already being written on the device.
    v0.1.1 therefore stays a testing build. Not because the judgement is known
    to be broken - it is not, any more - but because one pass is not yet the
    evidence a claim of this size needs.
    v0.1.0 claimed this fixed on unit tests alone and had to retract it. The
    difference here is that a phone said so. The caution is the same.

  11 words have no recording of the word itself. Pagkain, Tapon, Suot,
    Padyak, Alok, Apir, Hugas, Kinig, Sali, Tara and Salamin have no
    word_<word>.wav, so their entries in the voice bank are empty. Rachel
    speaks the sentence around the word and the word is shown as a caption
    while the pause is held, so the game stays playable and the scene keeps
    its timing - it is quieter at the moment that matters most. Ligpit, which
    was on this list, has its recording now.

  3 more voice bank entries are empty, for the Bahay scene that was dropped -
    14 unwired in all, out of 482.
    They are harmless: the scene is off the journey map, so nothing asks for
    them. They are left in rather than deleted, against the scene being built.

  The opening prompt of a scene holds a long silent pause before the child is
    allowed to speak. Unchanged from v0.1.0.

  The MINI GAME button on the main menu is drawn and clickable but does
    nothing. Deliberate: there is no mini game to open, and a button that
    loads a stub is worse than one that waits.

  A word badge sits low on the screen in Paaralan 5, at y = -350. Paaralan 2
    and Parke 4, also listed in v0.1.0, no longer do.

  No voice input without an internet connection; offline recognition is off.

  Suspend and resume behaviour has not been tested on a phone with the
    speaker at full volume. Unchanged from v0.1.0.

  The game cannot be answered by voice on desktop at all; the speech plugin
    is Android and iOS only. A desktop build is not released for this reason.

  The leaderboard shows every player on the device with no way to filter to
    one child. It scrolls now, which is what made ten profiles usable, but
    filtering and paging are still not implemented.

INSTALLATION / RUNNING INSTRUCTIONS
-----------------------------------

See README.txt in this folder. In short:

  1. v0.1.0 can be upgraded in place. Anything older than v0.1.0 must be
     uninstalled first - the package name changed at v0.1.0.
  2. Copy SALINLAHI-v0.1.1.apk to an Android 8.0+ device and open it.
  3. Allow installation from this source, then allow the microphone.
  4. Stay online, turn the volume up, and say the Filipino word on screen.

Confirm the build by opening SETTINGS > ABOUT; it should read v0.1.1.
